Questions

A few things people usually ask.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the scope, and the start date is confirmed in your quote. As a guide: a small refresh is often 1–2 weeks, a standard redesign 2–4 weeks, and a new site 3–6 weeks, once the deposit, content and access are in. Standalone SEO setup is typically one week.

What platforms do you build on?

Wix, Squarespace and WordPress — so you can keep updating the simple things after launch, without needing a developer for every small change.

Is there a free first step?

Yes. You’re welcome to a free 15-minute website review, or a free 30-minute discovery call if you’d like to talk through a project. There is no obligation either way.
