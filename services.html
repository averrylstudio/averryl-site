The core offer

Three ways we can work together.

Website design & development

New sites and thoughtful redesigns on Wix, Squarespace or WordPress. A review of your current site, updated design, clearer navigation, mobile-friendly pages, contact and booking checks, testing and launch. Basic on-page SEO is included with a redesign.

Social media marketing & management

A consistent presence that feels like your practice — visual direction, branded posts and support so you can show up without starting from a blank page each week. Quoted to the scope you need.

Brand identity & visual strategy

Logo, colour palette, typography, tone of voice and mood — so every page, post and form feels like the same calm, considered practice. Quoted to your scope.

Also available

SEO, intake forms and ongoing care.

SEO setup

A one-week audit of your current site and basic on-page SEO for your key pages, so people searching for the support you offer can actually find you. Also included as part of a redesign. Quoted to your scope.

Intake forms

Fillable PDF forms and online forms that are easy to complete on any device — including forms that sit inside SimplePractice, TherapyNotes, Jane, IntakeQ and similar. See form pricing.

Website Care

A monthly plan so your site stays looked after: hosting and platform help, backups and uptime checks, and small edits. A second plan adds one SEO, content or Google Business Profile update each month. See care plans.

A free 15-minute website review

A second pair of eyes on your current site — what’s working, and one or two gentle fixes. No obligation, and the offer is open whenever you’re ready.
